## Source boundaries

- [Nexus](https://nexus.eotir.com/rankchart.html/) supplies the captured display grid, not adopted source precedence. The rank-only workbook extract preserves compared titles, grades, original coordinates and strikeout markers. Payroll and personnel are excluded.
- [Combine saved family](https://holocron.swcombine.com/wiki/Ranks_of_the_Galactic_Empire) supplies ordered face colors from 88 embedded GIFs in references/combine-galactic-empire-ranks.mhtml. It is an older chart, distinct from the [current chart](https://www.swc-empire.com/ing/general/ranks). Neither establishes Imperial Republic titles or authority.
- [Whatsahonda comprehensive chart](https://www.deviantart.com/whatsahonda/art/Imperial-Rank-Table-991198927) supplies paired-row visual comparison via the local PNG; its live page was unavailable in discovery. It is a visual cross-check, not a source for exact arrays or official hierarchy.
- Source background colors are not tile color rules. Rows below read top to bottom; each row reads left to right. Gold and silver tokens describe candidate face appearances, never entitlement. Cylinders, heraldry, dress and appointments are separate.

## Nine specialty branches

All palettes below are proposed visual distinctions. They do not infer office powers, uniforms, privilege or a universal rank ladder. A neutral common palette with separate department accents is an alternative: easier shared grade recognition, weaker branch distinction, and fewer unique assets.

| Branch | Comparison family | Palette proposal and rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Inquisition | II | red becomes purple, gold stays gold. Distinguishes the institution from intelligence visually without inventing a legal entitlement. |
| Judiciary | II | red becomes white, gold becomes purple. A restrained pearl/purple comparison; judicial office is not equated with an intelligence organization. |
| Ministries | MI | green/gold source motif. Consistent saved institutional comparison, expanded beyond external industry ranks only where local titles exist. |
| Security Bureau | ISB | red/blue source bars. Preserves the comparison family; transparent second-row padding is excluded from arrays. |
| His Majesty's Personal Staff | PG | blue becomes cyan, orange becomes white, gold becomes teal. Provides a recognizable staff study without conflating personal appointment with military command. |
| His Majesty's Royal Guard | M | red becomes purple, blue becomes white, gold stays gold. Uses military geometry as a comparison; retains local Master Sergeant at O-1 and imports no military titles. |
| State | PG | blue/orange/gold. A central-government diplomatic comparison; local State titles and grades remain separate from the external government hierarchy. |
| Regional Governance | RG | blue/red/gold. Closest saved institutional comparison, with explicit nearest-pattern gap adaptations. |
| Intelligence Service | II | red/gold. Existing intelligence motif; lower enlisted-like positions have no corresponding saved II family and therefore use proposed grey/red studies. |

Missing reference-family rule: use the exact graphic if available; otherwise choose the nearest number in the same family, choosing the lower number on a tie. Higher E grades use grey over the last-row accent; higher C grades duplicate the last tile; other higher grades append the last tile per row. When the external family is absent, ME-1 or MHC-3 supplies a proposed starting motif. This deterministic fallback creates a design only for an already populated official cell; all 66 Nexus blanks remain blank. Equal patterns across different ranks are allowed proposals and should be considered during visual review; count alone is not a settled seniority code.
