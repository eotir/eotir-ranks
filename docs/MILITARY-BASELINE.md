## What agrees

## What stays open

- Shared upper tiers: Nexus HC-7 Praetor / Royal First Family, HC-6 High Councilor / Chief of Staff / Grand Minister, HC-5 Vice Chancellor / Royal Family. IRAF instead has HC-6 Praetor, HC-5 High Councilor and an ungraded G-26 HM Chief of Staff. Do not extend the intersection upward by choosing either scheme.
- Throne grades: Nexus RT-4/3/2/1 differs from the sheet's RT-3/2/1/1. Grand Vizier is visibly struck out in the sheet. Those rows are outside this initial military set.
- The Full Pay Scale combined Armed Forces ladder is a different historical structure. It does not override this branch-specific intersection.
- Research & Development, Military Criminal Investigative Service, Military Justice, Civil Service, Royal Fleet, Royal Guard, special operations and other branches can be researched later. Military first does not imply automatic inclusion of every armed or security institution.
- A grade hierarchy does not establish High Command heraldry entitlement, dress colors, code-cylinder issue/count, plaque placement, or an era of universal applicability.

## Consequences for plaque exploration

Current Combine and comprehensive fan charts have different numbers of officer/command/high/enlisted steps. Match designs explicitly to Imperial Republic records; never shift a rank to match an external badge chart.

For the first small comparison, sample low/middle/high grades and both branch-specific and shared titles. Include an enlisted example because the modern Combine page emphasizes officer/trainee coverage. If a grade has no selected design, leave it visibly unresolved. Before a complete chart, decide whether Marine and Army equal-grade titles share a badge or use branch-distinct patterns.

The value 69 describes occupied source cells. Shared designs can reduce asset count; alternate service/dress/era variants can increase it. Do not promise 69 approved plaques before the assignment model exists.
